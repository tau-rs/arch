Review and merge, on the shell

Re-seated. The MR belongs to a session; its review is the session focused in review: the left pane shows the session's Changes (Magit-style stages, viewed marks, progress), the center shows the hunks in one scroll as a tab beside the Map, the inspector holds the checklist and the verdict, gates and the pipeline sit on the Checks tab. A remark asks for a change or needs nothing; a change is a plan element realized in the session, never edited here. Merge is the same checklist with the gated button under it.
